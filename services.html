12:21:11FAQ

Pricing questions

Why does a retwist price depend on length and style?

Longer locs and detailed styles take more time. Each retwist price assumes 125 or fewer locs and 3/4 inch or less of new growth.

Can I change my style at the appointment?

Yes, if the new style takes equal or less time. The price stays at the original booking.

Do you have a consultation?

Yes. A 20-minute consultation is $25, and a loc consultation is mandatory before starter locs.

Are the times exact?

All style times are estimates. Plan for the time listed and arrive on time; late fees start at 10 minutes.

Found your style? Book it.

Booking is online through Acuity. A non-refundable deposit secures your time and goes toward your total.
