Please read before booking

Policies, in my words.

The same policies listed on my booking page, organized so you can find what you need fast. Booking an appointment means you agree to them.

Before

Booking & deposit

  • A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment, and it is applied to the final total of your service.
  • Remaining balance is due through Zelle, Cash, Cash App, Apple Pay or Afterpay.
  • ALL STYLE TIMES ARE ESTIMATE.
12+

Who can book

  • NO KIDS under 12 years old.
  • Hair must be 4 inches long.
  • No extra persons are allowed during your appointment.
24 hrs

Reschedule & cancel

  • Reschedules and cancellations must be made 24 hours prior.
  • One reschedule is allowed; after that, a new deposit is required.
  • No call, no show or same-day cancellation results in deposit forfeiture and 100% full payment, with no future bookings.
  • Appointments made within 24 hours are subject to the same policy.
  • A style change must be equal or lesser time, and the price stays the original.
+10 min

COMMUNICATION is KEY!

10minutes late: $10
15minutes late: $20
20minutes: reschedule
  • Running late? Text or call before your time. At 20 minutes the appointment is rescheduled, unless a shorter style is possible.
24 hrs

Sick policy

  • If you are sick, please be courteous enough to RESCHEDULE or CANCEL 24 hours PRIOR TO your appointment.
  • Cancellations and no-shows result in forfeiture of your deposit as well as 50% of the service being charged.
+30%

Emergency squeeze-in

  • Need an appointment outside normal hours, on a day off or last minute? An emergency fee of 30% of the style total applies, paid before scheduling.
  • Text (775) 487-1221: “I am aware of the emergency fee and need an appointment.”
24–48 hrs

Confirmations

  • You’ll get an email confirmation when you book.
  • A text reminder goes out 24 to 48 hours before your appointment.
Balance

Payment

The deposit is taken at booking. The balance is paid at your appointment with:

  • Zelle
  • Cash
  • Cash App
  • Apple Pay
  • Afterpay
Before · during · after

COME CAMERA READY!!!

  • Every client is photographed and recorded before, during and after the service for marketing.
  • Don’t want your face shown? Just let me know.
Media

Photos & artwork

  • Salon photos, videos and artwork may be used for marketing.
  • Prefer your face not be shown? See Come camera ready.
