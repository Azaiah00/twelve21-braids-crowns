I’m Vanessa, the stylist behind Twelve:21 Braids & Crowns: silk presses, knotless and feed-in braids, locs and sleek ponytails, off John Young Parkway in Orlando’s Princeton / Silver Star area.

12:21:07FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through Acuity at twelve21braidsandcrowns.as.me. A non-refundable deposit secures your appointment and is applied to your final total.

How much is a silk press?

A silk press is $120 to $165 depending on length and density, and takes about 2 to 2 hours 35 minutes. It includes a deep cleanse, condition, hydrate, blow out and press.

How much is a loc retwist?

Loc retwists run $100 to $225 depending on length and style, from just the top with no style ($100) to waist length with a combination style ($225). Shampoo is included.

Is hair included with knotless braids?

It depends on the style. Classy and Edgy knotless and Faux Locs include hair. For Miracle Knotless you bring 3 to 5 bundles, and for the Boho Bob you drop off your boho hair 24 hours before.

Do you braid kids?

Clients must be 12 or older, and hair must be at least 4 inches long.

What happens if I am running late?

Communication is key: 10 minutes late is $10, 15 minutes is $20, and at 20 minutes the appointment is rescheduled unless a shorter style is possible.

What payment methods do you take?

The remaining balance can be paid with Zelle, cash, Cash App, Apple Pay or Afterpay.

Where is the studio?

3701 John Young Pkwy, Suite 100-7, Orlando, FL 32804, off John Young Parkway in the Princeton / Silver Star area of northwest Orlando.
