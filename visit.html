12:21:01How booking works

Four steps to your chair.

  1. Pick your service

    Choose your service and time on Acuity. Not sure? The price finder shows exact retwist and silk press prices.

  2. Secure it with a deposit

    A non-refundable deposit holds your appointment and is applied to your total.

  3. Watch for reminders

    You’ll get an email confirmation, then a text reminder 24 to 48 hours before.

  4. Come camera ready

    Arrive on time with hair at least 4 inches long. Pay your balance by Zelle, cash, Cash App, Apple Pay or Afterpay.
